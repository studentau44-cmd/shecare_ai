🔬 Pregnancy & HIV/STI Support

Time-critical HIV PEP guidelines, responsible pregnancy test timelines, and STI education.

Time Critical Medical Protocol

🚨 HIV PEP (Post-Exposure Prophylaxis) 72-Hour Window

PEP is a 28-day emergency medication course that can prevent HIV from establishing in your body after potential exposure. It MUST be assessed and initiated as soon as possible, and strictly within 72 hours (3 days) of exposure. Every hour counts.

Where to get PEP: Any Hospital Emergency Department (ER), Sexual Assault Referral Centre (SARC), or One Stop Crisis Centre can evaluate and dispense PEP. Clinical assessment must not be replaced by a calculator.

🤰 Responsible Pregnancy Guidance & Testing Window

SheCare AI does not diagnose pregnancy from symptoms. Symptoms like nausea, tenderness, or fatigue can stem from shock, stress, or emergency contraception.

📚 STI Information & Clinical Testing Timelines

Most sexually transmitted infections (STIs) are asymptomatic in the early stages. SheCare AI cannot rule out infections based on symptoms. Clinical laboratory testing is required.
